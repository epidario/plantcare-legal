Delete Your Account

Request the permanent deletion of your PlantCare account and all associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to the address below
  2. Use the subject line: PlantCare — Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address associated with your account
  4. We will process your request within 30 days

Contact us at

plantcare.appdev@gmail.com

What will be deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, avatar)
  • All your plants and their details
  • All recorded operations (watering, fertilizing, etc.)
  • All photos uploaded to the app
  • Your houses and memberships
  • All preferences and settings

Important notes

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. If you shared plants in a house with other users, those plants will remain visible to other members unless they are also deleted.
