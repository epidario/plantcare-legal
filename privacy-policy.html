Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective date: May 2026

Your privacy matters to us. PlantCare is designed to help you take care of your plants — we collect only the data necessary to provide this service and never sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Who we are

PlantCare is an independent mobile application developed and operated by Dario Episcopo ("we", "us", "our"). If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at plantcare.appdev@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account information — email address, display name, profile photo (optional)
  • Authentication data — encrypted password or OAuth token (Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In)
  • Plant data — plant names, species, photos, care schedules, operation history
  • Location data — approximate location of your houses (optional, used only for weather data)
  • Usage data — app interactions, feature usage, error logs (anonymised)
  • Device data — device type, operating system version, app version

When you use the app as a guest (without creating an account), we collect only anonymised usage data. No personal information is linked to a guest session.

3. How we use your data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain the PlantCare service
  • Sync your plants and care history across devices
  • Send push notifications for watering reminders
  • Display local weather information relevant to your plants
  • Enable house sharing with users you explicitly invite
  • Identify plant species from photos (AI feature, Premium)
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Process Premium access requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the PlantCare service.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers, strictly necessary to operate the app:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — database, authentication, file storage. Servers located in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method. Privacy Policy
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — weather data. Only approximate coordinates are sent, no personal data. Terms
  • PlantNet (plantnet.org) — plant identification from photos (AI feature). Images are sent for identification and not stored by PlantNet. Terms

When you share a house with other users, those users can see your display name, profile photo, and the plants you add to the shared house.

5. Data storage & security

All data is stored on Supabase servers located in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest using AES-256 encryption.

We implement Row Level Security (RLS) policies to ensure that each user can only access their own data. Shared house data is only accessible to explicitly invited members.

Plant photos are stored in a private storage bucket and are only accessible to the owner and members of the house where the plant is shared.

6. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

You can request account deletion at any time by visiting our account deletion page or by emailing plantcare.appdev@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing of your personal data
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at plantcare.appdev@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

PlantCare is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of PlantCare after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
